Formatted text in the Properties panel points at the page. A rich text property shows a read-only preview of its words and an Edit on page button that selects the block, brings the field into view and puts the caret at its end; a property the block never marks as a field says it is not editable on the page. A short text with a limit shows how many characters are used. A needs-input marker on a missing text, and Enter on a selected block that has text, start typing on the page instead of opening Properties; the whole formatting flow can be done with the keyboard alone.
