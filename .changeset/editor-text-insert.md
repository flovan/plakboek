Rich text can be extended with structure from the page. Typing `/` at the start of an empty paragraph or after a space opens a menu of Paragraph, Title, Heading, Subheading, Bulleted list, Numbered list, Quote and Divider, limited to what the field's theme allows; typing filters it and Enter inserts. The toolbar gets an Insert menu with Quote and Divider, and in a narrow frame it keeps to the frame width: Done shrinks to its icon first, then Strikethrough, Underline and the lists move into a More menu.
